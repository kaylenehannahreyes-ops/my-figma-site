About me

in my own words

I'm Dr. Cristina Yang. I run cardiothoracic surgery at the Klausman Institute, and I'm very good at it.

Short version of why you want me on your team: I finished first in my class at Stanford with a 4.0, on top of a PhD from Berkeley. In residency I skipped the basics because I didn't need them. When the attendings had to pick one resident for the first solo surgery, they all picked me. That doesn't happen.

I'm not here to be liked. I'm here to be great, and to work with people who can keep up. Put me in an OR, a lab or a room full of nervous interns and I'll make it better, mostly by making it faster. I also know what I'm worth, so I won't pretend to be surprised when it shows.

Tell me I'm beautiful and I'll shrug. Compliment my brain. My hands are tiny little geniuses too, you can mention those.
